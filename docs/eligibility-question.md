
# Organizer Eligibility Question — NASA Data Requirement

> Owner: Member 1 (Coordination, Science, Pitch)
>
> Purpose: Obtain clarification about the NASA data requirement for Global Awards eligibility.

## 1. Background

Our project, CREW VITALS, is a prototype for astronaut health monitoring.

We are using the official challenge resources to understand NASA-recognized spaceflight health risks and research areas.

These resources include:

- NASA Human Research Program Risks.
- NASA HRP Five Hazards.
- NASA HRP Ongoing Research.
- Canadian Space Agency radiation monitoring information.
- Canadian Space Agency science in space information.

We are also investigating independently accessible NASA datasets, including:

- NASA OSDR Environmental Data Application (EDA).
- NASA OSDR RadLab.
- NASA NLSP Bedrest Campaign 1 Actigraphy.

The official challenge resources provide scientific and risk context, while the additional datasets may provide numerical environmental, radiation or analog measurements.

## 2. Question for the Organizers

For Global Awards eligibility, would our project satisfy the NASA data requirement if we use:

1. At least one of the NASA-related resources provided in the official challenge Resources section to inform our scientific approach; and
2. An independently retrieved NASA dataset, such as NASA OSDR EDA or RadLab, as an actual measured data source in the prototype?

Or must the numerical NASA dataset itself be one of the datasets explicitly linked in the official challenge Resources section?

We want to ensure that our implementation follows the competition's data eligibility rules correctly.

## 3. Additional Clarification

Would NASA Human Research Program risk information and evidence reports count as NASA data for this requirement, or must the project use a NASA dataset containing actual measurements or observations?

## 4. Our Current Approach

Until we receive confirmation:

- We will cite official NASA and CSA challenge resources.
- We will identify the origin of every dataset used.
- We will distinguish NASA-measured observations from simulated and analog data.
- We will not claim Global Awards eligibility based on an unconfirmed interpretation.
